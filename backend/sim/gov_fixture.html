Udyam Registration for Micro Enterprises

Registration is free of cost, paperless and based on self-declaration. Fee: Rs 0. No documents need uploading; only Aadhaar number is required.

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card of proprietor
  • PAN card for validation
  • Bank account statement for account details

Steps

  1. Enter Aadhaar number and validate with OTP.
  2. Validate PAN details against Income Tax database.
  3. Fill enterprise name, address and bank details, submit self-declaration.
  4. Download e-certificate with dynamic QR code. No renewal needed.

For help visit the Champions Control Room at your District Industries Centre.
