## Scope of responsibility

The DPO shall:

1. Be the point of contact for data principals exercising rights under
   Chapter III of the DPDP Act (access, correction, erasure, grievance
   redressal, nomination).
2. Advise the Organisation and its employees on DPDP obligations and monitor
   compliance (s.10(1)(a)).
3. Cooperate with the Data Protection Board of India on requests and
   inquiries (s.10(1)(b)).
4. Maintain the record of processing activities (see `DPDP_COMPLIANCE_MAP.md`)
   and oversee the breach process in `BREACH_RESPONSE.md`.
5. Act independently — no instruction on performance of these duties may be
   given by the Organisation (s.10(2)); the DPO shall not be dismissed or
   penalised for performing them.

## Platform duties the DPO oversees (Civic Pathfinder)

- Consent notices and purpose limitation for all personal data (app
  consent flows; `user`, `consent`, `vaultitem` tables).
- Erasure requests: account deletion path removes identity + vault data;
  the DPO confirms completeness against backups (retention ≤ 30 days).
- Grievance redressal: in-app grievance submission with statutory timelines;
  escalation to the DPO after 7 days unanswered.
- Annual review of this appointment, the breach procedure, and the DPDP map.
